# Art debt

Assets that hit the 3-iteration time-box and shipped as "simplest acceptable". Each entry: asset · what's wrong · what a fix would be.

- **CH_Nobody (iteration 3, M3.3: final)** — block fists replace the faceted spheres. Still no back-view tile in the review sheet (the kit renders front/side/¾); the back reads from the in-game camera every frame, so this stays a nice-to-have.
- **EN_Convict (iteration 2, M3.3)** — broken chains on the shackles; still the simplest body in the roster (fine for the swarmer). Convict and Slinger are the closest pair at 64 px (both slim); the Slinger's hood point and hanging sling plus its green carry it.
- ~~Look-dev mud patch~~ — replaced by `PR_MudPatch` (M3.1/M3.2).
- **M2.2 enemies (Pit Cur 240, Shield Thug 260, Slinger 272, Brute 440, Fire-Pot Thrower 276 tris; PR_SlingStone, PR_FirePot)** — first-pass quality, silhouettes pass at 64 px. M3.3: Brute (iteration 3) has spiked iron pauldrons and a sunk head, a shoulder line nothing else has; Fire-Pot Thrower recoloured only (it was at its 3-iteration cap) to soot black/ash so it separates from the pit's browns; Shield Thug has a lighter shield over a darker body. Lineup at 64 px: `Screenshots/blender/lineup_sil64.png`.
- **Slave Pit kit (M3.1, iteration 1–2)** — terraces and ramps are very low-poly (22 / 8 tris) and read as clean slabs rather than packed earth; fine for readability, a jitter/erosion pass could add character later. Fire pit flames are static cones (no flicker).
- **EN_Gorm (iteration 2)** — reads by size and the flail ball; the whip is small from the front and the beard/head merge with the hunch at a distance. A third pass could enlarge the whip coil and add a lighter skin tone to the head.
- **EN_BeastLord (iteration 3, Phase 5.8: final)** — reads from the front by the lion hood's spiky mane ruff and from any angle by the 4.1 m goad. From the ¾ view the hood and the mane heap merge into one rust-and-ochre mass, so that angle relies on the goad. A further pass could lift the hood's snout and ears clear of the mane, or give the pelt a lighter belly.
- **EN_Murmillo (iteration 2), EN_NetThrower, EN_WarBoar (iteration 1), Phase 5.8** — pass at 64 px. The Murmillo's first bronze scutum read as wood against the sand and is now blue; the Net-Thrower's net bundle is small from behind (the trident carries it); the boar is a box body with tusks, readable by its low wide shape and its charge.
- **Stone Amphitheater kit (Phase 5.8)** — the seating bowl's eight tiers are one flat-shaded mesh with travertine faces (iteration 2: the first pass read dark brown from the floor). The stage wall's niches are flat recesses, with no statues in them.
- **CH_MurderMachine (iteration 2, Phase 5.9)** — 808 tris. Iteration 1 was almost all dark iron (too close to the Blood Knight) and hid his arms inside the barrel; iteration 2 has a rust barrel and the bare arms out past it. The cleaver is held low in front, so from the camera behind him it shows only during the chop (over his head); a rest pose with it on his shoulder needs animation work (BACKLOG). The cleaver icon took 2 iterations (the first read as a face).
- **CH_ThunderChampion (iteration 2, Phase 5.10)** — 788 tris. Iteration 1 read all brown (bronze, skin and leather) and its crest was too small for 64 px; iteration 2 has lighter bronze, a bigger blue cape and a crest twice the size. The crest runs side to side, so from the side it's a thin bar; the armour is still close to the Nobody's browns from the front (the blue cape and the crest separate them). The Storm Spear icon is iteration 1 (its blade is small).
- **CH_GoldenIdol (iteration 2, Phase 5.11)** — 844 tris. Iteration 1's gold read ochre and its sash poked out past the shoulder; iteration 2 uses the light gold for the body and keeps the sash inside. The palette's golds are still warm-mustard rather than mirror-bright; a specular "gleam" would need a separate material (the palette rule says no).
- **CH_AshenOracle (iteration 2, Phase 5.12)** — 584 tris. Iteration 1's hook bent backwards and a dark smoke cone on the censer read as a spearhead; iteration 2 hangs the censer from a forward crook. He's grey on the amphitheatre's pale sand, so he reads by shape more than colour. The censer orb he lobs reuses the Bomb Satchel's `WP_Bomb` mesh (a censer-orb mesh would be nicer). The masked stranger uses the Oracle's own model.
- **CH_NetWeaver (iteration 2), WP_Trident (iteration 2), the Trident icon (iteration 2), Phase 5.14** — 872 + 156 tris. Iteration 1 slanted the trident backward, lost a bronze galerus against the tanned skin and the rope net, and blacked the face out with a full beard; iteration 2 tilts the trident forward, makes the galerus a bigger steel plate and keeps a chin beard. At 64 px he reads by the forward-slanted trident more than the galerus. WP_Trident's barbed points first faced the shaft (the kit's `cylinder` tapers toward +Y); the icon's first prongs fanned out like a fork. The Cast Net reuses the thrown Net's `WP_Net` mesh scaled to 3.2 m: sparse at that size (a denser net prop would be nicer).
- **CH_Beastmaster (iteration 2), CH_Wolf (iteration 1), EN_BeastHandler (iteration 2), Phase 5.15** — 720 + 252 + 268 tris. The Beastmaster's first pass was too dark on dirt and its hood's ears too small at 64 px: a lighter jerkin and taller ears. The wolf reads by its cyan collar against the curs' magenta; it's drawn ×1.3 (`WP_WolfPack.meshScale`) to read in a crowd, and its tail is a stiff stick. The handler's first pass was one brown box (dark skin under a long apron, an edge-on whip coil): lighter skin, a shorter apron, a front-facing coil and a lash across his chest. His own silhouette is still generic: he reads by the two curs he walks.
- **Grand Arena kit (Phase 5.13)** — iteration 1 for the stands, towers, patron boxes, the monumental gate and the statue. The floor and the terraces took 2 iterations: v1's white/grey checker on the processional ways was busy right where the horde walks (now white marble with a grey joint every third row), and the terrace tops came out all gold. The flame took 3: a rust spire, then a yellow that bloomed past the hazard colour, now an orange-red sheath with a yellow tip. The blade took 2: its orange band showed on one side only. There's no city beyond the stands: the sky starts at the back walls.
- **EN_GildedMercenary (iteration 2), EN_Sorcerer (iteration 1), Phase 5.13** — 288 and 284 tris. The mercenary's first axe head was edge-on from the front and vanished at 64 px, and he was 360 tris; now the blade is broadside and the helm unbevelled.
- **EN_GildedPatron (iteration 3, Phase 5.13)** — 920 tris. Iteration 1 hid the bearers under the litter; iteration 2 hid his head behind his belly. Now the bearers stand at the poles' ends and the canopy is raised. The bearers are small next to the litter, and he throws from a fixed hand height.
- **UI frames (U1, `PixelSrc/ui_frames.py`, iteration 3)** — the 30 frames took three passes (the ansata read as a candy wrapper, the banner's hem was a shallow zig-zag, the laurel clumped). Still weak: the 20×24 laurel branch is drawn at 2.75× beside VICTORY! and looks chunky (a 2× branch would fix it, BACKLOG); marble veins only in the corners; an ansata 40 tall squashes its handles; the purple cloth's creases barely show.
- **P3 UI art (`PixelSrc/ui_frames.py`): IconAureus (iteration 3), ArrowLeft/Right (iteration 3), IconSkullGalea (iteration 3)** — the aureus took three passes (a keyhole, then a horse-like mane): at 32×32 it reads as a gold coin with a head in relief, but the laureate profile is suggestive, not a portrait. The pilum arrows read as a cross, then a fish, before the barbed head on a long shank; small at 48×36. The skull's crest went from a flat block to a fan. The glow, the seal, the heart and the gilded wreath passed in one or two.
