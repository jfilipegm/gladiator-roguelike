# Art debt

Assets that hit the 3-iteration time-box and shipped as "simplest acceptable". Each entry: asset · what's wrong · what a fix would be.

- **CH_Nobody (iteration 3, M3.3: final)** — block fists replace the faceted spheres. Still no back-view tile in the review sheet (the kit renders front/side/¾); the back reads from the in-game camera every frame, so this stays a nice-to-have.
- **EN_Convict (iteration 2, M3.3)** — broken chains on the shackles; still the simplest body in the roster (fine for the swarmer). Convict and Slinger are the closest pair at 64 px (both slim); the Slinger's hood point and hanging sling plus its green carry it.
- ~~Look-dev mud patch~~ — replaced by `PR_MudPatch` (M3.1/M3.2).
- **M2.2 enemies (Pit Cur 240, Shield Thug 260, Slinger 272, Brute 440, Fire-Pot Thrower 276 tris; PR_SlingStone, PR_FirePot)** — first-pass quality, silhouettes pass at 64 px. M3.3: Brute (iteration 3) has spiked iron pauldrons and a sunk head, a shoulder line nothing else has; Fire-Pot Thrower recoloured only (it was at its 3-iteration cap) to soot black/ash so it separates from the pit's browns; Shield Thug has a lighter shield over a darker body. Lineup at 64 px: `Screenshots/blender/lineup_sil64.png`.
- **Slave Pit kit (M3.1, iteration 1–2)** — terraces and ramps are very low-poly (22 / 8 tris) and read as clean slabs rather than packed earth; fine for readability, a jitter/erosion pass could add character later. Fire pit flames are static cones (no flicker).
- **EN_Gorm (iteration 2)** — reads by size and the flail ball; the whip is small from the front and the beard/head merge with the hunch at a distance. A third pass could enlarge the whip coil and add a lighter skin tone to the head.
- **EN_BeastLord (iteration 3, Phase 5.8: final)** — reads from the front by the lion hood's spiky mane ruff and from any angle by the 4.1 m goad. From the ¾ view the hood and the mane heap merge into one rust-and-ochre mass, so that angle relies on the goad. A further pass could lift the hood's snout and ears clear of the mane, or give the pelt a lighter belly.
- **EN_Murmillo (iteration 2), EN_NetThrower, EN_WarBoar (iteration 1), Phase 5.8** — pass at 64 px. The Murmillo's first bronze scutum read as wood against the sand and is now blue; the Net-Thrower's net bundle is small from behind (the trident carries it); the boar is a box body with tusks, readable by its low wide shape and its charge.
- **Stone Amphitheater kit (Phase 5.8)** — the seating bowl's eight tiers are one flat-shaded mesh with travertine faces (iteration 2: the first pass read dark brown from the floor). The stage wall's niches are flat recesses, with no statues in them.
