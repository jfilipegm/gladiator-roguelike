# v0.10.0 — The Murder Machine
A fourth gladiator: the arena's executioner, a huge armoured slab of a man with a cleaver to match. Beat Gorm to unlock him.

## Downloads
| File | Platform | Notes |

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and two 10-minute bot runs as the Murder Machine on the dev build (the Slave Pit and the Stone Amphitheater), with no errors. They held 384 and 380 fps on average, and 197–199 fps with 250+ enemies. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Murder Machine** (unlocked by beating Gorm; if you already have, he's waiting at the next main menu):
  - **The biggest gladiator:** a tall pointed executioner's hood, a barrel of rusty plate, and 150 HP. He's slow: 85 % move speed.
  - **Executioner's Cleaver:** he raises it over his head and brings it down in front of him, toward the nearest enemy. Everything where it lands is crushed and thrown back, and a pale ring runs out across the sand. Anything right next to him gets hit too.
  - **Executioner's Plate:** every hit he takes is 4 lower (never below 1), so the horde's small hits barely scratch him. Nothing knocks him back: not boars, not bosses. Big hits still hurt, though, and burning still burns.
  - **Verdict:** the bar under his health fills only from hits he takes. When it's full ("JUMP!"), his next landing from a jump is the Verdict: "VERDICT!", a 7 m shockwave that hits everything round him and staggers whoever survives for a second.
  - The Executioner's Cleaver also joins everyone's level-up pool.
- **The Blood Knight now unlocks when you reach the Stone Amphitheater.** That's the original plan: "beat Gorm below 30 % HP" was a stand-in until a third arena existed. If you already have him, you keep him.
