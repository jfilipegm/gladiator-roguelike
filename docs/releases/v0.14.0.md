## What's new
- **The Grand Arena** (unlocked by beating the Beast Lord):
  - **The shape:** a cross of white marble, four arms 52 m wide reaching 70 m from the middle. Patron boxes full of nobles sit in the corners between the arms, and a monumental gate ends each arm. The floor is ~1.4× the Stone Amphitheater's.
  - **Fire jets:** a row of nozzles crosses each arm. Every few seconds one row glows orange-red, then roars a wall of fire. It's **too tall to jump**: time your crossing. It burns the horde too.
  - **Blade sweepers:** four blades at shin height around the dais in the middle. When one spins up its circle glows; **jump it** or stay out. It mows the horde as well.
  - **More of the horde:** up to 400 at once here, and they come faster (the other arenas are unchanged).
  - **Tougher:** enemies have +80 % HP and you take +35 % damage. Renown × 2.5.
- **Two new enemies:**
  - **The Gilded Mercenary:** a sellsword with a halberd. His hits **steal your gold**; kill him and he drops everything he took.
  - **The Sorcerer:** a tall pointed hood and an orb staff. He opens a rune under your feet that erupts a moment later, and **blinks away** when you get close.
- **The Gilded Patron**, the arena's owner, carried in on a litter by four bearers:
  - **Coin Bombs:** purses that burst on and around you and scatter real gold.
  - **Golden Volley:** a fan of red-hot coins. The statue and the dais block them.
  - **Shove:** his bearers barge forward.
  - **His guard:** every so often he hires mercenaries, who jump down from the patron boxes nearest you. **While any of them lives, he takes much less damage** (gold threads, and a gold "GUARDED" bar). Kill the guard, then hit him.
  - **Phase 2:** every jet fires at once, and the blades spin for the rest of the fight.
  - When he falls, his fortune is yours.
