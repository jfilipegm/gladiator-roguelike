# v0.15.0 — The Net-Weaver
An eighth gladiator: a retiarius, a fisherman who fights with a net and a trident. He gathers the crowd into one spot and holds it there. To unlock him, win a run with a spear in your hands.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and two 10-minute bot runs as the Net-Weaver on the dev build (the Slave Pit and the Stone Amphitheater), with no errors. They held 387 and 366 fps on average, and 182–199 fps with 250+ enemies, and the bot beat Gorm. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Net-Weaver** (unlocked by winning a run while holding the Pit Spear, or its evolution, the Impaler):
  - **Looks:** a bare-headed fisherman with a cyan headband, a steel guard rising over his left shoulder, a net slung down his back like a cape, and a trident. 95 HP, a little faster than the Nobody.
  - **Trident**, a new kind of weapon: three quick jabs fanned out at the nearest enemy. Up close, all three land on the same enemy; further out, they spread across three.
  - **Cast Net:** start a slide with the net ready (every 4 s) and he flings a net 5 m ahead. Everything it lands on is **hauled into the middle and held** for 2 s. Bosses can't be held, only slowed.
  - **Caught:** his hits do +50 % damage to anything held (Rooted) and +25 % to anything Slowed, with every weapon he picks up.
  - The Trident also joins everyone's level-up pool once he's unlocked.
