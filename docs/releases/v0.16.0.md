## What's new
- **The Beastmaster** (unlocked by killing 25 Beast Handlers, over all your runs):
  - **Looks:** a hunter in a wolf-head hood with the ears up, a fur mantle, a hunting horn across his chest. 100 HP.
  - **Wolf Pack**, a new kind of weapon: **wolves fight beside you** for the whole run. Each one hunts the nearest enemy near you and bites; they never stray far from you, and enemies ignore them. Levels add wolves (up to five) and make them bite harder and faster.
  - **Feral Bond:** when you're hit, your wolves frenzy for 4 s: they bite much faster and run faster.
  - **Hunting Horn:** start a slide with the horn ready (every 5 s) and every wolf **pounces** on the enemies ahead of you: three bites in one, and they're staggered. If there's nothing to hunt, the horn waits.
  - The Wolf Pack also joins everyone's level-up pool once he's unlocked.
- **A new enemy, the Beast Handler** (the Stone Amphitheater from 2:10, and the Grand Arena from 1:30): a whip-cracking handler who walks **two Pit Curs on leashes**. They can't get far from him; **kill him and they're freed** and run off.
- **Since v0.15.0,** the gladiator select screen slides to keep your pick in view (there are more gladiators than fit the screen), and long descriptions fit their cards.
