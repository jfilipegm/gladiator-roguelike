# v0.17.0 — The Imperial Arena
A fifth arena, the biggest floor yet: the Emperor's hippodrome, where chariots race round the spina, the Praetorian Guard marches in formation, and the Emperor's own champion waits behind the triumphal gate.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and four 10-minute bot runs in the Imperial Arena on the dev build, with no errors. They held 273–311 fps on average and 153–170 fps with 250+ enemies on screen (up to 405 at once), and every run had 8 chariot races. The first run found that the Emperor's Champion couldn't catch a player who keeps running; he's faster now, and he fought the bot in the other three runs. None of them finished him in the time left (his best was 38 %), so his health is a first guess. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Imperial Arena** (unlocked by beating the Gilded Patron):
  - **The shape:** a long hippodrome of travertine and imperial purple, 1.3× the Grand Arena's floor. A row of arched starting stalls closes the west end, a semicircle of stands closes the east, and the triumphal gate stands at its apex. The Emperor watches from his box on the north side.
  - **The spina:** a raised stone walkway down the middle, with stairs on both sides, an obelisk, gold turning posts at both ends and a lap counter of seven gold eggs. Terraces in the outer fields give more high ground.
  - **Chariot races:** from 1:20, every minute, trumpets sound and the whole track (the darker loop on the sand) glows orange-red for 3 seconds. Then two four-horse chariots burst out of the starting stalls and run two laps, trampling everything on the track. **Get off the track, or jump** a chariot as it passes. They mow the horde down too.
  - **Tougher:** enemies have +115 % HP and you take +45 % damage. Renown × 3.
- **Two new enemies, who come together:**
  - **The Centurion:** an officer with a side-to-side red crest and a vine staff. He leads a line of five Praetorians.
  - **The Praetorian:** a guardsman with a tall purple shield, which blocks most of what hits it from the front. The line keeps its places as it marches, stops just short of you and **shoves** together.
  - **Kill the Centurion** and his line breaks: they stagger and drop their shields for a few seconds.
- **The Emperor's Champion**, the Emperor's own gladiator, with an eagle on his helmet, a spear, a scutum and javelins:
  - **Spear Thrust:** a lane fills, then he lunges down it. **Shield Bash:** a cone, then a big knockback; his shield blocks half of what hits him from the front. **Javelin Volley:** a fan of javelins at your height, so the spina is no shelter, but the obelisk is.
  - **Phase 2, "PRAETORIANS, TO ME!":** two formations march in through the gates nearest you, and more keep coming. He also charges with his shield: lure him into the obelisk to stun him.
  - **Phase 3, "FOR THE EMPEROR!":** the chariot races run back to back, and he gets faster. **A chariot hits him hard too**, so lead him across the track.
  - When you're far away, he strides to close the distance.
