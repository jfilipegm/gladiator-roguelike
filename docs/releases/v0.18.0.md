# v0.18.0 — The Colosseum
The sixth arena and the top of the ladder: the Colosseum, where the floor itself opens under the horde, the dead don't stay down, and the arena's first champion rises to meet you.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and a 10-minute bot run in the Colosseum on the dev build with no errors. It held 286 fps on average and 147 fps with 250+ enemies on screen (up to 461 at once). The bot brought the Eternal Victor to 3 % in his third phase but didn't finish him. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Colosseum** (unlocked by beating the Emperor's Champion):
  - **The biggest floor yet:** a great oval of sand, 1.3× the Imperial Arena, ringed by three tiers of packed stands under red-and-white awnings, the Emperor's box, and the Gates of Life and Death at both ends. A round stage stands in the middle, guarded by bronze statues; two hills give high ground.
  - **The hypogeum:** under the sand runs a ring of 30 trapdoors. From 0:40 a group of them shakes (orange-red) and drops open for a few seconds: anything standing there falls to its death. You don't fall: you're thrown off, hurt. Lifts raise packs of enemies from below.
  - **Tougher:** enemies have +155 % HP and you take +55 % damage. Renown × 3.5. The biggest waves of the ladder.
- **Two new enemies:**
  - **The Skeletal Legionnaire:** when it dies it collapses into a bone pile, and a few seconds later it stands up again at half health. Run over the pile to scatter it, burn it, or drop it through a trapdoor.
  - **The Chimera:** a lion, a goat and a snake in one beast. It plants and breathes a cone of fire that also burns any enemy in the way.
- **The Eternal Victor**, the Colosseum's first champion, rises from the stage with a giant falx:
  - **Reaper's Circle** (jump the ring), **Victor's Lunges**, the **Falx Throw** (out and back along a lane; the statues stop it) and the **Leap**.
  - **Phase 2:** every trapdoor opens and his dead come up the lifts; the **Grasp of the Dead** holds you in place.
  - **Phase 3:** the doors stay open, the light fades, and he chains his attacks.
  - **He doesn't die the first time.** Destroy his remains before he rises again.
