Everything from your playtest of v0.17.0: new gladiator and arena screens, closed arenas with no safe spots, a clean death, a stats page, and a new kind of card with a price.

## What's new
- **New gladiator and arena screens:**
  - **The roster:** every gladiator is a square portrait on a bone *tessera*, the token a Roman gladiator earned. Locked ones are black silhouettes; pick one to read its hint.
  - **The wax tablet** on the right shows the portrait, weapon, traits, and your runs and victories with that gladiator.
  - **The arena ladder** climbs from the Slave Pit at the bottom to the Colosseum at the top, with a picture of each arena, its boss, size, hazards and difficulty.
  - **The Codex's GLADIATORS tab** uses the same roster.
  - Mouse, keyboard and gamepad all move in two dimensions, and nothing slides off the screen any more.
- **A stats page:** pause (Esc) to see every stat and where it comes from: your gladiator's base, each card, evolutions, synergies and traits (and the admin menu's buffs in the dev build, in pink).
- **Previews:** at a level-up, the stats sit beside the cards. Hover or select a card, an altar or a chest to see what changes ("Attack Speed 100% → 110%"): green for gains, red for losses.
- **Vows, cards with a price:** eight new cards in a red-and-gold frame that raise one thing and cost you another, e.g. Berserker's Vow (+35% damage, −20% max HP), Heavy Plate (+15% armor, −12% move speed), Gambler's Vow (+20% luck, −1 reroll). About two level-ups in five offer one. Banish the ones you don't want.

## Fixes
- **No more safe spots.** The onlookers' platforms in the Slave Pit had been built 3 m inside the wall; they're back where they belong, out of reach. Every arena's wall is now one closed loop, and the game never lets you outside it. The pillar tops, the shed roof and other high spots throw you off. The horde can now reach you even when you stand right against the wall.
- **The arenas stay closed:** behind every gate is a pen or a barred tunnel, so an open door shows where the enemies come from, never a way out.
- **The Bookmaker** takes your wager on **E**, like a chest, and tells you what it costs ("WAGER 30 GOLD", or "NEED 30 GOLD").
- **Death:** your weapons, wolves and projectiles stop the moment you die, and nothing counts after it.
- **The crowd** gives one last groan (or cheer), then falls silent on the end screen. It also hushes while the game is paused.
- **The admin tag** now says what it means ("ADMIN: GOD MODE · BUFFS ON (F1)"), in the corner.
- **The synergy chips** under your Boons no longer cover their own names.
