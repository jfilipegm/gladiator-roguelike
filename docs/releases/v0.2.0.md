# v0.2.0 — The Crowd's Favor
The crowd now plays along. A **Favor** meter (the gold bar under your level, top right) fills as you put on a show. When it's full, the crowd throws you a gift. If the show gets dull, the Arena Master steps in.

The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **Favor:** it rises with:
  - kill streaks the announcer calls out;
  - elite kills;
  - killing a variety of enemy types;
  - kills while sliding or in the air;
  - coming back from very low HP;
  - stretches without getting hit;
  - Gorm's entrance and his second phase.
- **Crowd gifts:** each one lands 3–6 m away, marked by a gold ring. Walk over it before it disappears (25 s):
  - **Healing amphora:** restores 30 % of your HP.
  - **Gold pouch:** a shower of 20 coins.
  - **Laurel wreath:** a burst of XP.
  - **Sword in the sand:** a weapon you don't have, at level 4, for 30 seconds. It fights beside your weapons and doesn't use a slot.
- **The Arena Master's whims:** if you avoid the fight (few kills and nobody near you for a while), the crowd grows restless. Then either a gate opens next to you, or a **champion** arrives with a gold ring under it. Kill the champion within 20 s for Favor and gold. Whims never happen in the first 1:30, during the boss, or more than once a minute.
- **New sounds:** the gift throw, the gift landing and a crowd boo.
- **Fix: crit builds no longer stutter.** Every crit used to freeze the game for a split second, with no limit. With a crit-heavy build, the game spent a quarter of the run in slow motion, so the 8-minute clock fell almost 2 minutes behind. Crits and elite hits now share one freeze every 0.8 s at most.
- **Fix:** the admin menu's "Swarm now" / "Elite now" stopped working after toggling the Director off and on.

## Known issues
- All sounds are synthesised placeholders.
- How often gifts and whims happen are first guesses (`Assets/Data/Config/FavorConfig.asset`).
- The Windows build was smoke-run under Wine, not on real Windows. The macOS build hasn't been run on a Mac.
