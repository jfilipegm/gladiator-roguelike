The last gladiator of the roster: the minotaur chained under the Colosseum since its first games. Beat the Eternal Victor to set him free.

## What's new
- **Asterion, the Chained** (unlock: defeat the Eternal Victor). Half man, half bull, chained under the stage for four hundred years. 130 HP, a little slow, no armour.
  - **The Labyrinth Chain:** every 1.6 s he hauls the chain round in a 240° sweep, 5.2 m long, from one side of the nearest enemy to the other. Everything it passes is hit and shoved sideways. **The stone** at the end always crits and shoves harder, so fight at the chain's length. Shields only stop it if they face the way it comes from. Levels add a **backswing** (straight back the other way) and later a third sweep.
  - **Gore (passive):** run into enemies head-first and your horns toss them out of your path.
  - **Rage:** a bronze meter under your HP fills while the crowd presses round you (and when you're hit). When it's full you're **Unchained** for 8 s: a bellow throws everyone back, the chain whirls all the way round, you're faster, nothing knocks you back, and you gore everything you run into. Then you're **Spent** for 2 s. It isn't a button: the rage decides.
- **The chain joins the level-up pool** once he's unlocked, for every gladiator.
- **A tease:** while he's still locked, the Eternal Victor's fall is followed by a new line under the sand.
- He has his own portrait, Codex page and tablet on the select screen.
