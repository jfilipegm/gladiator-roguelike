# v0.25.0 — Your notes on the Roman look
Every note from your v0.24.0 playtest: an inventory beside the level-up cards, a new pause screen, a cleaner HUD with Roman icons, a banner that flaps instead of swaying, an arena grid, and no more perch on the cages.

## What's new
- **Your items while you level up.** An inventory column on the left mirrors your stats on the right, and the cards stay centred between them.
  - It has 4 weapon slots and 6 Boon slots; empty slots stay visible.
  - Hover a slot to see what it does.
  - Select a card for something you own, and its slot lights up with its details.
  - A card for something new shows a faded "ghost" of its icon in the next free slot, with its details.
- **The stats column no longer jumps.** Its rows stay in place while you move between cards; only the numbers change.
- **The pause screen is the level-up's frame.** PAUSED on top; RESUME, OPTIONS and MAIN MENU in the middle (Esc, O, Q as before); your items on the left and your stats on the right. Hover a stat to see where it comes from. Clicking empty space no longer resumes.
- **The HUD.**
  - New 8-bit icons: a heart, a Roman gold coin (an aureus) and a skull in a legionary's helmet with a red crest.
  - Every icon is centred on its bar or number, with even padding inside the plates.
  - The level reads "LEVEL 2" inside its plate.
  - The XP bar is 15 % taller.
- **The announcer banner** still drops and rolls back up, but no longer sways left and right: a wave runs across the cloth as it unrolls, and the words ride it.
- **Buttons** glow gold around their own shape when selected (no more yellow rectangle).
- **The main menu.** The title is a gold word between laurels instead of the big mosaic box. RENOWN is readable on its plate. New Codex entries are marked with a red wax seal stamped "N".
- **Gladiator select.** No yellow ring on the picked gladiator: a gold-and-green laurel wreath crowns the shield and it lifts. The skin arrows are bronze pilum tips.
- **The arena select is a grid**, three arenas per row, like the gladiator grid, so more arenas fit later.
- **Everywhere:** text keeps the same padding inside every frame, and every label passes a contrast check against what it sits on. The Daily Games' read-only edicts page no longer shows ON chips.
- **Fixed:** standing on the edge of a cage (or a training post, or any other tall prop) kept you out of the horde's reach. You now slide off every such top.
