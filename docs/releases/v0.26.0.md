# v0.26.0 — The Bookmaker's book
Your note on the Provincial Pit's Bookmaker: press E at his booth and he opens his book of wagers, from a 5-gold small bet to a 40-gold high-stakes duel with his champion.

## What's new
- **The Bookmaker's book.** In the Provincial Pit, step into his gold ring and press **E**: the game pauses and his book opens. Pick a wager with the mouse, 1–4 or the gamepad, or **Walk away** (Esc) for free.
  - **I · Small bet:** 5 gold, kill a few in 30 seconds (below your recent pace). Wins 15.
  - **II · Fair bet:** 10 gold, beat your own pace in 30 seconds. Wins 35.
  - **III · Long shot:** 20 gold, the same kills without being hit once. Wins 80.
  - **IV · High stakes:** 40 gold, he sends his champion (an elite) in through a gate: kill it within 45 seconds. Wins 200.
  - Your kills this run open the bigger wagers (100, 300, 600), and Luck can open one more early. A wager you can't pay shows what it needs.
  - He takes one wager at a time and wants a minute between them ("COME BACK LATER").
  - The secret's reward (the Loaded Dice Boon) now needs a win at the fair bet or bigger.
