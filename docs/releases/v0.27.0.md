# v0.27.0 — The new look, and the Playground
Every character in the game is redrawn in the style you picked (lineup variant A): chibi proportions, simple chamfered shapes, flat-shaded facets, readable faces. Same gameplay, same hitboxes, same animations. And admin mode in every build, with a Playground to see and test everything.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suites, a 300-enemy stress run, and in-run captures. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The whole cast in the new style:**
  - all 11 gladiators, the Wolf and the Bookmaker;
  - the 18 enemies;
  - the 6 bosses and the Chimera;
  - all 20 skins;
  - the select-screen portraits and the codex pictures.
- **Faster with big crowds.** Each character is now drawn in 6 pieces instead of 20–90. At 300 enemies the game ran at 336 fps instead of 181 on the test PC: frame time 2.98 ms instead of 5.52, and the slowest frames 4.0 ms instead of 10.7.
- **The old look is kept in the project.** One menu item in the Unity Editor switches every model back if a character reads worse.

- **Admin mode in any build.** On the main menu, type **A-D-M-I-N**. A gold ADMIN tag appears top-left, and the game remembers it until you type it again. Launching with `-admin` works too.
  - **What it unlocks:** every gladiator, skin and arena can be picked. This doesn't touch your save: nothing gets unlocked or bought for real.
  - **F1** opens the admin panel in any arena.
  - In the Linux dev build, F1 works as before, and the menu shows your real progress until you type ADMIN.
- **The Playground.** In admin mode, **PLAYGROUND** under PLAY → pick anyone, in any skin → FIGHT!
  - A big flat field with the test slope, stairs, ramp and platform.
  - **The showroom:** every gladiator with its skins, every enemy by tier and every boss at full size. They're labelled and loop through idle, run, attack and hit, so you can walk around them.
  - Nothing spawns on its own, there's no timer, and god mode is on.
  - Nothing you do there counts toward your save.
- **New in the admin panel (F1):**
  - **FIGHT A BOSS:** one button per boss. It loads that boss's own arena with you as you are, and the boss walks in about 3 seconds later.
  - **FREEZE:** enemies stand still and can't hurt you, but they still take hits and die. Good for looking up close or testing a weapon.

## What to look at
- Each gladiator on the select screen, and in a run: running, swinging, getting hit.
- The enemies in a crowd: can you still tell them apart at a glance? The enemy colour patches are still there.
- The bosses' special moves still play: Gorm's flail, the Beast Lord's spear, the Emperor's eagle wings and the Eternal Victor's falx throw.
- The artists' own doubts:
  - the Bookmaker's quill can read as a blade;
  - Asterion's snout looks pig-like from the front;
  - the Brute's head sits on his shoulders;
  - the Centurion's crest looks like a red umbrella;
  - Gorm's beard looks like a bib;
  - the Beast Lord's front is busy;
  - the Skeletal Legionnaire's helmet looks like a cap.

## Known issues
- The joints are rigid parts, as before: an arm turns at the shoulder as one piece.
- Sounds are still placeholders made in code, as before.
