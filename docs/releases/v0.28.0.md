## What changed
- **The gladiator select screen** draws properly again after typing ADMIN. The shields and plates were drawn washed out because the screen was rebuilt while hidden.
- **The pause menu:** each word is centred on its plate.
- **The NEW seal** is a round disc with the N in the middle. It's smaller and sits on the text line, right after the word. Say if you want it before the word instead.
- **The gold titles** (Marcellus): the dark outline is thinner, so the letters read gold instead of dark.
- **Tougher bosses** (your note: "the bosses are too easy"):
  - a boss's HP grows with your level when the fight starts (+2.5 % per level above 20, so about +37 % at level 35; less for the Gilded Patron and the Emperor's Champion, whose fights were already long);
  - every boss hits 25 % harder: melee, javelins, coins and bombs;
  - more HP: the Provincial Champion 13 000 → 15 000, the Beast Lord 15 000 → 24 000, the Eternal Victor 22 000 → 34 000;
  - shorter pauses between attacks for Gorm, the Champion, the Beast Lord and the Eternal Victor.

How much longer, measured with the test bot (it never dodges and picks random cards, so it hits 2-3x softer than you do). The test run stops at 10:40, which cut three of the fights short:

| Boss | v0.27.0 | v0.28.0 |
|---|---|---|
| Gorm | down in 95 s | down in 77 s, with a much stronger build (154 damage/s instead of 106) |
| The Provincial Champion | down in 89 s | 30 % HP left after 108 s |
| The Beast Lord | down in 52 s | 42 % HP left after 92 s |
| The Eternal Victor | down in 68 s | 54 % HP left after 86 s |

With your builds, expect fights of roughly 50-90 s, the Eternal Victor the longest. Tell me which ones are now too much.
