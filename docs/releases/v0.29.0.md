# v0.29.0 — Your notes on v0.28.0
Fixes from your playtest of v0.28.0: the boss title, the Armory Chest, the Blood Slide, and four gladiators reworked
(the Ashen Oracle, the Net-Weaver, the Thunder Champion and the Beastmaster).

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suites, in-game captures of every change. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app that nobody has run on a Mac yet.

## What changed
- **The boss's name** now takes the place of the big BOSS word, in the same lettering and size, and the line under it is gone. His HP bar shows his HP in numbers, like yours.
- **The Armory Chest** is Roman now, and each arena has its own: a crude weapon crate in the Slave Pit, a chest with a red legion shield for a lid in the Provincial Pit, an iron legion strongbox with a gold eagle in the Stone Amphitheater, a marble chest with a gold laurel in the Grand Arena, the shield chest again in the Imperial Arena, and the eagle strongbox in the Colosseum.
- **Blood Slide** leaves a real smeared blood trail on the sand. It dries darker and fades, instead of pink rings.
- **The Ashen Oracle** throws a cursed purple fireball. It curves after its target and bursts on the first enemy it touches, cursing everything around it. No more goblin-style lob.
- **The Net-Weaver:**
  - his trident is a new, chunky three-pronged model;
  - it lunges out and back, hitting three lanes at once;
  - his net now flies at the thickest crowd near you, opens as it lands and holds everything under it for 3 seconds.
- **The Thunder Champion:**
  - a lightning-bolt icon (with SLIDE under it) shows when the blink is ready, fills while it recharges, and pops when it's back;
  - the blink leaves a lightning streak and after-images, and the camera glides after you instead of cutting.
- **The Beastmaster** starts with a **Hunting Spear** next to his wolves. It's a quick jab that marks what it hits, and the wolves go for marked enemies first. The pack now stays near you, fights what's close to you, moves on after a bite or two, and follows when you run.
