# v0.3.0 — The Pit Goblin
A second gladiator, and a secret: something rattles in the Slave Pit.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and a 10-minute bot run playing the goblin on the dev build. Gorm arrived on time and fell; the run had no errors. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Pit Goblin**, a second gladiator. It's locked at first: you have to find it (no spoilers in the game, but see below).
  - **Tiny and fast, but fragile:** 70 HP, 112 % speed, −10 % damage, a longer pickup reach.
  - **Bomb Satchel:** it lobs a bomb into the thickest group of enemies nearby. A cyan ring shows where it lands, then it explodes after a short fuse. The goblin's bombs never hurt the goblin. Levels add a second and a third bomb, a bigger blast, more damage and a shorter fuse.
  - **Scavenger:** every 25 seconds it pulls a junk weapon out of its sack: a weapon you don't hold, lent for 15 seconds. It never replaces a weapon the crowd threw you.
  - **Trap Slide:** every slide leaves a snare behind you (3 at most). The first enemy to step in is held for 2 seconds and bleeds. Bosses can't be held, but they bleed.
- **How to unlock it** (spoiler): one crate in the Slave Pit **rattles** when you're near it. Walk into it, or press **E** beside it. The discovery is saved immediately, even if you die afterwards. Once unlocked, the Bomb Satchel can also come up in level-up cards for the Nobody.
- **Gladiator select:** pick with the mouse, the arrow keys or the d-pad. Locked gladiators show a hint.
- **New sounds:** the goblin's cackle, a snare snapping, a crate rattling, a bomb going off.

## Known issues
- All sounds are synthesised placeholders.
- The goblin's numbers are first guesses (`Assets/Data/Gladiators/GL_PitGoblin.asset`, `WP_BombSatchel`, `TR_Scavenger`, `TR_TrapSlide`).
- The menu's background still shows the Nobody when you pick the goblin.
- The Windows build was smoke-run under Wine, not on real Windows. The macOS build hasn't been run on a Mac.
