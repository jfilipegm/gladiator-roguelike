# v0.4.0 — The Blood Knight
A third gladiator: a fallen knight who heals on kills and hits hardest when he's nearly dead.

## Downloads
| File | Platform | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `Gladiator-v0.4.0-linux.tar.gz` | Linux x86_64 | Extract it, then run `./Gladiator.x86_64`. |
| `Gladiator-v0.4.0-windows.zip` | Windows 10/11, 64-bit | Extract it, then run `Gladiator.exe`. SmartScreen may warn about an unknown publisher: choose **More info → Run anyway**. |
| `Gladiator-v0.4.0-macos.zip` | macOS (Intel and Apple silicon) | Unzip it and move `Gladiator.app` anywhere. The app isn't notarised, so the first time, **right-click → Open → Open**. If macOS still says the app is damaged, run `xattr -cr Gladiator.app` in Terminal. |
| `Gladiator-v0.4.0-linux-dev.tar.gz` | Linux x86_64 | The same game plus the **F1 admin menu**. |

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and a 10-minute bot run playing the knight on the dev build. It had no errors and held 239 fps with 250+ enemies; Gorm was down to 10 % when the run ended. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Blood Knight**, a third gladiator. Blackened plate, a horned helm, a crimson cape, and a greatsword as long as he is tall. He's locked at first.
  - **Slow and tough:** 120 HP and 92 % speed.
  - **Crimson Greatsword:** a slow, huge cleave (220°, 3.4 m) whose hits make enemies bleed.
  - **Bloodthirst:** kills heal him (1 HP each, at most 6 HP per second; elites heal 8). All other healing (regen, amphorae, healing cards) is **halved**.
  - **Bloodlust:** the lower his HP, the harder he hits, up to +60 % damage at 20 % HP or less. The HUD shows the bonus.
  - **Blood Rite:** kills and wounds fill a crimson **Blood meter** under the HP bar. When it's full, the HUD says **SLIDE!** Slide to pay 12 % of your max HP for 8 seconds of frenzy: +40 % damage, +25 % attack speed, and kill heals doubled. The Rite never takes your last HP.
- **How to unlock him:** beat Gorm with **less than 30 % HP** left. His greatsword then also joins everyone's level-up cards. The plan was "reach the third arena", but the game has only one arena so far, so this stands in until then.
- **The announcer's important lines stay readable:** in a big fight, kill-streak and crowd lines no longer wipe out a line like "BLOOD RITE!" or a boss beat in its first second.
- **New sound:** the Blood Rite (a heartbeat and a rush).

## Known issues
- All sounds are synthesised placeholders.
- The knight's numbers are first guesses (`Assets/Data/Gladiators/GL_BloodKnight.asset`, `TR_Bloodthirst`, `TR_Bloodlust`, `TR_BloodRite`, `WP_CrimsonGreatsword`).
- The menu's background still shows the Nobody when you pick another gladiator.
- The Windows build was smoke-run under Wine, not on real Windows. The macOS build hasn't been run on a Mac.
