# v0.5.0 — The Provincial Pit
A second arena: a real one this time, with stands full of townsfolk, traps that hurt everyone, two new enemies and a champion behind a shield the size of a door.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, two 10-minute bot runs in the Provincial Pit and one in the rebuilt Slave Pit on the dev build. They had no errors and held 178–198 fps with 250+ enemies (196 fps in the new Slave Pit, where the bot also beat Gorm). In the second run, the Champion called his guards twice, charged into stone twice and was at half HP when the run ended. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Provincial Pit**, a second arena. A ~50 m sand arena inside a stone wall, with wooden stands of townsfolk (~200) and three gates, so the horde comes from three sides.
  - **How to unlock it:** beat Gorm once. After you pick a gladiator, the menu then asks **which arena**. With only the Slave Pit, FIGHT! still goes straight in.
  - **Tougher:** enemies have +25 % HP and you take +15 % damage. The horde arrives sooner and more mixed. Renown counts it as a deeper arena (×1.5).
  - **Spike traps:** six iron grates. Each one flashes orange-red, then spikes shoot up for a moment. **They hurt everyone**, and ordinary enemies take double, so lure the horde over them. Jump to clear them.
  - **Barricades and braziers:** wooden barricades split the floor into lanes. Weapons don't break them; the Champion does.
  - **Stone columns and raised platforms** (two with stairs, one with a ramp) to block, climb and slide around.
- **Two new enemies** (in the Provincial Pit):
  - **Shield Archer:** hides behind a tall wicker shield that blocks hits from the front. A thin orange-red line shows where it's aiming; the line freezes just before the arrow flies, so step aside then. Its shield dips for a moment after each shot.
  - **Assassin:** fast and fragile. It circles to your side instead of your face, crouches behind an orange-red lane, then dashes through you. Sidestep or jump the lane; afterwards it stands winded for a second, which is your chance.
- **The Provincial Champion**, the new boss. A murmillo behind a huge curved shield that blocks 90 % of hits from the front, so get to his side or back. Bleed and burn get through.
  - **Shield Bash:** an orange-red cone in front of him, then a slam with big knockback.
  - **Gladius Flurry:** three quick thrusts (five in phase 2), each with its own lane; step aside each time.
  - **Shield Charge:** a wide lane, then he charges. He **smashes every barricade** in his way, and braziers fall into burning ground. If he hits a **column or the wall**, he's stunned for 2 seconds with his shield down and takes +50 % damage. Lure him into the stone.
  - **"TO ME, BROTHERS!":** every 25 s he calls Shield Archers and Shield Thugs through the gates.
  - **Phase 2, "FOR THE PROVINCE!":** at half HP he roars, every spike trap fires at once, he attacks faster and charges twice in a row.
- **Gorm** fights exactly as before; his whip and charge lanes are now drawn as solid lanes.

## Fixes from your playtest
- **The Slave Pit is rebuilt, twice as wide:** an 84 × 70 m oval stockade on open ground instead of a pit in the void.
  - **The wall:** log palisades with three log gates, patched fences, dirt embankments with a wattle fence on top, and plank platforms full of onlookers.
  - **A yard where people work and train:** training posts, straw dummies, a practice weapon rack, a sparring ring, a water trough, hay, sacks, a woodpile, a tool shed, cages by the south wall, and trodden paths and worn circles in the dirt.
  - **You can't fall off any more:** invisible walls behind the whole boundary stop you, however many air jumps you have.
  - **A world outside:** hills, woods, the slaves' huts, the slavers' longhouses and tents, watchtowers and carts on the roads.
  - Gorm's burning fence follows the new wall.
- **The crowd sounds like a crowd**, not a steam train: dozens of voices talking, with shouts, claps and whistles. It swells when you get a multi-kill and settles into a slower, quieter murmur when you stop fighting.
- **Upgrade icons:** every card has an animated 8-bit icon (it bobs and shines) on an aura in its rarity's colour: grey common, green uncommon, blue rare, gold legendary. The weapon and Boon slots under your HP bar show the icons instead of letters.
- **The Pit Goblin:** you can now click a gladiator's card to pick it (the cards ignored the mouse), and the gladiator standing in the menu background changes to your pick. The Goblin had been unlocked in your save by my own test sessions, not by you; that is fixed, and your save no longer has him: find the rattling crate in the Slave Pit to free him. A backup of your old save is next to it: `save.before-goblin-fix.json`.
