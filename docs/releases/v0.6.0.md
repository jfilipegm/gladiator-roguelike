# v0.6.0 — Evolutions
Your weapons can now evolve into something stronger, and owning three upgrades of one kind switches on a synergy. A run has two new goals: finish a weapon, and commit to a theme.

## What's new
- **Weapon evolutions.** Get a weapon to **level 8** and own its **partner Boon** (any level). The next **Armory Chest**, Blood Altar or boss reward then offers its evolution as its first card, a gold **EVOLUTION!** card. The evolved weapon takes the old one's slot and keeps all its levels; you keep the Boon. Level-ups never offer evolutions, so chests matter late in a run.

  | Weapon | + Boon | = Evolution | What it does |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | Rusty Gladius | Lucky Cut | **Champion's Gladius** | Every swing is answered by a second one behind you. More damage and crits. |
  | Pit Spear | Long Reach | **Impaler** | Half again as long; whatever it runs through is pinned in place for 2 s. |
  | Javelins | Static Charge | **Thunderbolts** | Every javelin that lands arcs lightning to three more enemies. |
  | Chakram | Quick Hands | **Twin Moons** | A second disc follows the first on the other side. |
  | Bomb Satchel | Kindling | **Fire-Pot Barrage** | Every blast leaves the sand burning for 3 s. |
  | Gorm's Flail | Brute Force | **Chain of Ruin** | One more ball, bigger, and every hit slows. |
  | Crimson Greatsword | Open Wounds | **Sanguine Reaver** | A wider cleave of blood follows every swing; two Bleeds a hit. |

- **The cards tell you what goes with what:**
  - a weapon's card says which Boon evolves it;
  - a partner Boon's card says "Evolves your …" (or "open a chest!" once the weapon is maxed);
  - in the HUD, a weapon ready to evolve pulses gold and reads **EVO!**, and an evolved one reads **EVO**.
- **Synergies.** Every upgrade has tags (FIRE, BLOOD, LIGHTNING, CRIT, HEAVY, DEFENSE…), and weapons count now. Own **3 different upgrades** with the same tag and its synergy switches on for the rest of the run. The announcer calls it, and it gets a chip under your Boons. A new card shows its progress, e.g. "FIRE 2/3: Wildfire".
  - **Wildfire** (FIRE): every second, each burning enemy sets a neighbour alight.
  - **Bloodbath** (BLOOD): a bleeding enemy that dies sprays Bleed on everything around it.
  - **Storm Front** (LIGHTNING): every 4th hit forks lightning to 3 enemies. Thunderbolts is the third LIGHTNING upgrade, so this one comes late.
  - **Executioner** (CRIT): a crit on an enemy below 20 % HP kills it outright. Never a boss.
  - **Juggernaut** (HEAVY): +20 % area, +40 % knockback, +10 % damage.
  - **Bulwark** (DEFENSE): +8 % armor, +15 % max HP, +0.5 HP/s.
- New 8-bit icons for every evolution and synergy, and new models for the evolved spear, javelins, chakram, fire-pots and flail balls.

## Known issues
- **Balance is a first guess.** Evolutions land in the last minutes, right before the boss, and may make him easy (concern C16).
- **Hard to get:** finishing a weapon and finding its Boon depends on luck, and a run is 8 minutes. If it never happens for you, say so: the fix is in the numbers (weapon level costs, chest prices).
- **The Pit Goblin's weapons and the Blood Knight's greatsword** evolve too, but the goblin's improvised weapons (Scavenger) don't.
- Sounds are still placeholders made in code, as before.
