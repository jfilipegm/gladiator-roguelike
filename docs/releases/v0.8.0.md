# v0.8.0 — The Provincial Pit, Rebuilt
The second arena is now bigger than the first, as it should be on the climb to the Colosseum, and it's no longer round. The spike traps also come up where they're drawn.

## Downloads
| File | Platform | Notes |

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and two 10-minute bot runs in the new Provincial Pit on the dev build, with no errors. Both held 197–228 fps with 250+ enemies, and in one of them the bot beat the Champion. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Provincial Pit, rebuilt.** It was a ~49 m circle, smaller than the Slave Pit. Now it's a provincial town's walled square:
  - **An elongated octagon:** 100 × 70 m of sand with the corners cut off. That's about 1.4× the Slave Pit's floor and 3.3× the old Provincial Pit's.
  - **Four gates**, one in the middle of each side, so enemies come from every direction. The Champion still enters from the north.
  - **More to use:** six raised platforms (five with stairs, one with a ramp), a colonnade of pillars around the central dais, twelve barricades, ten spike traps and six braziers.
  - **About 400 townsfolk** in the stands, twice as many as before.
- **Spike traps fixed.** The spikes used to come up half off their grate, poking out past two of its edges. They now rise inside the grate. The orange warning before they fire is now the grate's own square, not a bigger circle around it. The damage area was always the grate itself, so now what you see is what hurts.

## Known issues
- **The bigger floor is a first pass.** Enemies spawn on the stretch of wall nearest you (within 55 m), so they don't spend half a minute walking over from the far end. The enemy cap is still 300, though. Tell me if it feels empty or too slow to build up.
- Sounds are still placeholders made in code, as before.
