# v0.9.0 — The Stone Amphitheater
The third arena: a real stone amphitheatre with thousands in the seats, beasts in the pens and a new boss, the Beast Lord. Beat the Provincial Champion to unlock it.

**Tested:** Linux: the full test suite, and two 10-minute bot runs in the Stone Amphitheater on the dev build, with no errors. They held 385 and 478 fps on average, and 201–251 fps with 250+ enemies and 1,700 spectators. In one of them the Blood Knight beat the Beast Lord. The Windows build is smoke-run under Wine on Linux, not on real Windows. The macOS build is a universal app (Intel and Apple silicon) that nobody has run on a Mac yet. Please report anything that breaks.

## What's new
- **The Stone Amphitheater** (unlocked by beating the Provincial Champion):
  - **A D shape:** a curved bowl of stone seating facing a straight stage wall, 120 × 88 m. That's about 1.4× the Provincial Pit's floor, so the climb keeps getting bigger.
  - **About 1,700 spectators** in eight tiers of seats.
  - **Four ways in for the horde:** the great gate in the stage wall and three tunnels through the seating.
  - **More to use:** a raised stage in two halves along the stage wall, twelve columns and four statues to break the archers' line of sight, and four raised platforms.
  - **Harder:** enemies have 50 % more HP, you take 25 % more damage, and an elite comes every 60 s instead of 90. Renown is doubled.
- **Beast cages.** Four cages sit in the stage wall. From 1:15, every 40 s one of them glows orange-red and rattles, then lets out a pack of **War Boars**. A boar shows its lane, then charges straight down it and tramples **everything** in it, the horde included. Step aside, or lure the stampede through the crowd.
- **Three new enemies:**
  - **Murmillo:** a big blue door-shaped shield that blocks 90 % of hits from the front. Get round the side.
  - **Net-Thrower:** lobs a net onto where you're standing (an orange-red ring shows where). If you're on the ground inside when it lands, you're caught: you slow to a crawl for 1.2 s. Keep moving, or jump.
  - **War Boar:** comes only from the cages.
- **A new boss, the Beast Lord:** a huge hunched man in a lion's pelt with a hooked goad taller than he is.
  - **Goad Sweep:** a wide cone in front of him.
  - **Hook Thrust:** a long lane that hits and slows you.
  - **Pounce:** a circle follows you, then stops. He leaps onto it and is slow to get up, which is your window to hit him.
  - **His horn** opens the cage nearest him every 20 s. At half health he opens every cage at once and pounces twice in a row.
  - When he falls, his beasts scatter.
- **The run summary names the right boss.** It said Gorm in every arena.
