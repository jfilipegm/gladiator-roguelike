### What changed, by finding
- **3, the Bookmaker on E** (`Bookmaker.cs`, `Hud.cs`): he now takes the wager only on Interact (E / gamepad North) inside
  his gold ring, like a chest. The old 1.2 s stand-in-the-ring channel is gone (walking through the ring could take 30 gold
  without asking; `channelTime` and `Channel01` are removed). His ring pulses while you're in it. While he calls you
  (within 9 m), a world prompt floats over his head: **"[E]  WAGER 30 GOLD"** in gold, or **"NEED 30 GOLD"** in red. In
  the ring, the bottom prompt reads "[E] Wager 30 gold: beat your own pace, win 90" (red "(not enough gold)" when short).
  E without the stake gets his "NO GOLD, NO WAGER." line. If a chest is in reach at the same time, the chest takes the
  press (one press never spends gold twice). `Interactables.cs` / `InteractableConfig.cs` didn't need changes.
- **4, nothing fights after death:** a new `RunState.CombatOver` = the player is dead **or** the run is over. It covers
  the frame of death too: `End()` only runs in that frame's Late tick, after the weapons and projectiles have ticked.
  The player-side damage sources that check it:
  - `WeaponSystem.cs`: `Halted`. The swing in progress never lands; jabs, echoes and extra strikes are dropped; the
    player's attack pose is released. A flail's balls (Orbit) stop at their angle and fall to the sand. Companions hold.
    `CompanionHit` is refused.
  - `CompanionPack.cs`: new `Hold()`. The wolves stand where they are and turn to the body. A pounce in the air lands
    without biting. They don't vanish.
  - `PlayerProjectiles.cs`: anything in flight (javelins, chakrams, bombs before they blow, coins, bolts, shockwaves)
    fizzles out at once in a small cyan puff (`maxFizzles` 24). The fallen flail balls are still drawn.
  - `BoonEffects.cs` (a player-side damage source not in anyone's list, so I took it): no procs, bursts, trails or
    spreads once combat is over. `RunEndController` now clears it on a death as well as on a win.
  - Traits with an effect still running: `CastNetTrait` (a net already in the air lands harmless), `TrapSlideTrait`
    (laid snares never snap), `ScavengerTrait` (no new loans).
  - The other traits only fire on an event and already refuse when `IsDead`, so they're unchanged: LightningDash,
    SmokeVeil, Verdict, HuntingHorn (its pounces go through the held pack), MakeItRain (its coins are projectiles),
    BloodRite. FeralBond, Stormcaller, Bloodlust, Bloodthirst, GoldenStreak, MidasBody and ExecutionersPlate deal no
    damage.
  - `RunState.cs`: kills, damage dealt, gold (in and out) and XP stop counting **from the moment the player is dead**,
    which is one Late tick before `End()`. That's stricter than the contract and still satisfies it. `MarkBossDefeated`
    still keys on `Ended` only, so a boss that falls in the same frame still counts.
  - The death beat itself is unchanged (0.3× slow motion, collapse, burst).
- **5, the crowd** (`SfxPlayer.cs`):
  - **The end:** on `OnRunEnd` the crowd gives one last reaction: the Boo slowed to 0.8× (a groan) on a death, the Cheer
    on a win. It's forced past the repeat limits so it's always heard. The murmur and roar hold for `endHold` 0.35 s,
    fade to 0 over `endFade` 1.5 s (real time, so it runs on the paused end screen), then the loops are **stopped**.
  - **Pauses:** while `TimeController.IsPaused` (level-up cards, pause menu, admin panel), both loops duck to
    `pauseDuck` 0.25 over `duckTime` 0.3 s, and they come back on resume.
- **6, the admin tag** (`Hud.cs`): it's spelled out, e.g. **"ADMIN: GOD MODE  ·  BUFFS ON  (F1)"** (also TIMELESS and
  SPEED x1.5). It's smaller (18 pt instead of 22, 85 % alpha) and sits in the **bottom-right corner**. Nothing appears
  under the timer any more.
- **9, the synergy chips** (`Hud.cs`): the icon is 24 px at 4 px from the chip's left edge; the name starts at 33 px. Each
  chip is exactly as wide as its name (+9 px). The chips flow in rows of up to 380 px, which wraps after two

  11 characters each; the test uses Executioner.

- The groan is the Boo clip at 0.8× speed: there's no groan clip, and adding one needs `AudioSetup` / `CrowdSynth`

- The floating Bookmaker tag shows across his whole call range, not only in the ring: it says what he's offering as
