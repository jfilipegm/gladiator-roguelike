### Decisions (one line each)
- The contract's "−2 armor" (Featherweight) and "+4 armor" (Heavy Plate) don't fit: armor is a fraction clamped at
  0–75 % (`PlayerHealth`). A cut costs a gladiator with no armor nothing, and +4 % is far too small. So Heavy Plate gives
  +15 %, and no price lowers armor or regen (negative regen does nothing either). Featherweight costs damage instead.
- Greed's Vow costs XP, not "enemies +10 % HP": enemy HP is `EnemyManager.hpMul` (nobody's file in P1). A price nothing
  read would be a fake downside.
- Prices are multiplicative (×0.8, not −20 % in the percent pool), so they're always felt and can never take a stat
  to 0.
- Vows stay out of rewards: a chest you paid gold for, or blood at the altar, should be pure upside.
- "Base" names the gladiator's own number in the sources (the page header names the gladiator).
- The page is one rich-text TMP block rebuilt only when its inputs change (stats version, build version, offers,
  selection, admin buffs, weapons). Sizes are relative, so auto-size (13–21 pt) shrinks a big build to fit.
- `LevelUpPanel` creates the `StatsPanel` at runtime (or uses the scene's), so no arena scene needs rebuilding.
- The page sits on the right in both faces: the HUD strip is top-left, and the admin tag (B) is in the bottom-right
  corner below it.
