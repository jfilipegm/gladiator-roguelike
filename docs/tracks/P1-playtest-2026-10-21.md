## The findings (the producer's words in quotes)
1. Gladiator select: the card row strands the mouse. → R9.
2. **Safe perch** (`pt_2.webp`): "the player is in a position where he can hit the mobs but they cant hit the player".
   In the Slave Pit the player stood on the onlookers' plank deck (`SM_YardPlatform`, `PieceRole.Solid`, ~2.4 m up).
   - Enemy melee needs `|dy| < 1` (`EnemyManager` ~970 and ~995), so anything higher that enemies can't climb is safe.
   - The likely route in: along the walkable berm's crest. `SlavePitLayout` gives each 48-section boundary piece its own
     wall box, at different distances (palisade 1.0 m out, fence 1.1, berm 2.3, platform 0.6, gate 1.3). Where the
     distance steps, nothing closes the side.
3. **Bookmaker:** "i tried going to him and pressing E like the chests but it did nothing". Today the wager is taken by
   standing in his gold ring for 1.2 s with at least 30 gold; with 0 gold he shows "NO GOLD, NO WAGER.".
4. **After death the weapons keep swinging** and killing (`pt_3.webp`). `RunEndController` plays a death beat at 0.3×
   slow motion before the pause, and `WeaponSystem.Tick` only stops at `dt <= 0`.
5. **The crowd sound** "keeps playing in a loop" on the death/end screen. `SfxPlayer`'s murmur (`_crowd`) and roar
   (`_roar`) loops never stop.
6. **"BUFFS" under the timer** (`pt_4.png`) confused the producer. It's the dev build's tag (`Hud.cs` ~448, an F1 admin
   buff is non-zero).
7. **Out of the arena** (`pt_5.webp`): "i shouldnt be able to go back here in the Slave pit". The player got behind
   the onlookers' platform, among the crowd. Same cause as 2.
8. **The gates show a way out** (`pt_6.png`): "the arenas must remain closed, the player should be able to see outside
   of it but never give the idea he can leave". The Slave Pit's `SM_YardGate` doors swing open (`ArenaGates`) while
   enemies come through, onto the open road.
9. **The synergy chip "Executioner" looks broken** (`pt_7.png`; "Juggernaut" in `pt_3.webp`). In `Hud.cs` ~181 the
   chip's icon sits 2–30 px from its left edge while the name spans 17–127 px, so the icon covers the first letters.
10. **Stats in game:** "there should be a way the user can see the stats of their gladiator in game, meaning it should
    show hp, dmg, speed, crit etc and how much are the buffs contributing".
    - **10b:** "those stats should also be shown when you go to pick a buff or in the altars and stuff and when you
      mouse hover it it shows the advantage or disadvantage".
11. **Trade-off cards:** "there should be buffs like that, stats that lower your stats, or up your damage but lower
    something else and positive ones, this so that the banish button has some usefulness and also to allow the player
    to make compromises sometimes". Today no card has a downside.

## Parts (three workers, no file owned by two)
### A — arena integrity (findings 2, 7, 8)
- **Owns:**
  - `ArenaBuilder.cs`; the boundary and gate code in every `Assets/Editor/Setup/*Layout.cs` (Slave Pit, Provincial Pit,
    Amphitheater, Grand Arena, Imperial Arena, Colosseum);
  - a new runtime clamp (e.g. `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/Arena/ArenaBounds.cs`, plus the call from the player's motor:
    say which file in your notes);
  - the gate-back art: `BlenderSrc/environment/` (new pens/tunnels, or additions to the arenas' scripts) and their FBX;
  - new tests.
- **Boundaries:**
  - Build each arena's invisible boundary as one **closed polyline**, joining every step between neighbouring wall
    pieces.
  - Add a last-resort **XZ clamp** of the player to the boundary polygon (O(1)–O(n) per frame, no GC), so no collider
    leak can ever let them out.
- **Safe perches:**
  - Nothing the player can stand on may sit more than ~1 m above the ground enemies can reach next to it, unless
    enemies can climb it (ramps or stairs) or it's outside the clamp.
  - Find every such spot in all six arenas: an EditMode/PlayMode audit that samples standable surfaces against the
    enemies' height grid and play area.
  - Fix each one: unreachable, sloped off, or reachable by enemies.
- **Gates:**
  - Behind every gate, a closed holding pen or tunnel, so an open door shows where enemies come from, never a way out.
    - Slave Pit: a spiked-log pen of waiting convicts, closed at the back; its roads start beyond the pens.
    - Stone arenas: a dark tunnel with a barred back.
  - Check each arena's gate models and say which already read closed.
- **Done when:**
  - `Tools/ccheck` errors=0.
  - A PlayMode test walks the player round every arena's whole edge (berm crests and platform tops included) and
    asserts they stay inside.
  - The audit test is written, and new models are reviewed (3 angles + 64 px).
  - The notes list what the orchestrator runs (layout regenerations, scene builds) and what to screenshot.

### B — run end, sound and small HUD fixes (findings 3, 4, 5, 6, 9)
- **Owns:**
  - `Bookmaker.cs` and the interact prompt code the chests use (`Interactables.cs`, `InteractableConfig.cs`);
  - `WeaponSystem.cs` and the player-side damage sources that must stop on death (traits under `Gladiators/`,
    companions, player projectiles: list them);
  - `RunState.cs`, `RunEndController.cs`, `SfxPlayer.cs`;
  - `Hud.cs`;
  - new tests.
- **The Bookmaker:** works with **E** like the chests, with the same kind of world prompt ("E  WAGER 30 GOLD", and
  "NEED 30 GOLD" when you're short). Keep the ring as his spot.
- **Death:**
  - On death, weapons, traits, companions and player projectiles stop at once.
  - `RunState` stops counting kills, gold and XP once `End()` is called.
  - The death beat itself stays.
- **The crowd:** at the end, one last reaction (a groan on death, a cheer on victory), then the murmur and roar fade to
  silence over ~1.5 s. They duck while the game is paused (level-up, pause menu) and come back on resume.
- **The admin tag:** spell it out ("ADMIN: BUFFS ON (F1)", "GOD MODE (F1)", …), smaller, in a corner, away from the
  timer.
- **Synergy chips:** the name starts after the icon, each chip is as wide as its name, and there's a small gap under
  the boon row. Check the longest synergy name.
- **Done when:** `Tools/ccheck` errors=0, PlayMode tests are written (the weapons stop on death, the kill count
  freezes, the crowd volume reaches 0, the Bookmaker takes the bet on E), and the notes say what to screenshot.

### C — stats page, previews and Vows (findings 10, 10b, 11)
- **Owns:**
  - `StatBlock.cs`, `PlayerStats.cs` (a source id on every modifier, and a per-stat breakdown);
  - a new `StatsPanel.cs` (UI), `LevelUpPanel.cs`, `CardText.cs`;
  - the altar and shrine code and their prompts (say which files);
  - `UpgradeDef.cs`, `UpgradeRoller.cs`, `UpgradeConfig.cs`;
  - a new `Assets/Editor/Setup/VowSetup.cs` and the Vow assets;
  - new tests.
  - Not `Hud.cs` (B owns it): the stats page is its own panel. It opens with the pause menu (watch the pause state)
    and beside the level-up cards.
- **The stats page:** each stat's total (HP, damage, attack speed, area, crit chance and damage, move speed, pickup
  range, luck, armor, regen, and the rest of `StatId`). Under it, the sources: the gladiator's base, each card, the
  evolutions, synergies and traits, temporary effects, and admin buffs (dev builds only, marked).
- **Previews:**
  - Hovering or selecting a card (level-up), an altar or a shrine choice shows each affected stat as
    "Damage 34 → 41": green for gains, red for losses.
  - Gamepad and keyboard focus show it too.
- **Vows (the trade-off cards):**
  - A new card family named after the gladiator's oath, about 8 of them, e.g. Berserker's Vow (+35 % damage / −20 %
    max HP), Heavy Plate (+4 armor / −12 % move speed), Glass Edge (+25 % crit damage / −15 % max HP), Featherweight
    (+20 % move speed / −2 armor), Greed's Vow (+40 % gold / enemies +10 % HP).
  - Rarity scales the upside more than the downside.
  - `UpgradeDef.IsVow`, its own split red/gold card frame, and a pool weight that makes them common enough to matter
    without flooding.
  - Banish works on them as on any card.
- **Done when:** `Tools/ccheck` errors=0; EditMode tests (breakdown sums equal the totals, Vow rolls and weights, card
  text shows the downside) and PlayMode tests (the page opens on pause, a hover preview matches the stat after the
  pick) are written; the notes say what to screenshot.
