## Surfaces and contrast (note 8; C builds the audit, B builds to it)
Each frame kind that text sits on has a **surface tone**: the colour of its face's middle in `ui_frames.py`, times the
Image's tint. A label passes when the WCAG 2 contrast ratio of its face colour against that tone is **≥ 3:1**. Labels
with no surface under them (over the 3D scene or the sky) must use `TextLook.Outline` (or the Shout look). Today's
light/dark split, for orientation (C's table is the source of truth):

| Light faces: dark ink | Dark faces: light ink (`ink`, `waxInk`, `marble`, gold) |
|---|---|
| AnsataBronze (`bronzeInk`), AnsataMarble and Marble (`marbleInk`) | Bronze (the panel's dark plate, e.g. the RENOWN plate and the HUD plates), Wax, Mosaic, Stone, the card insides, Scutum, Clipeus, the vexillum cloths, Trough, Chip, KeyChip |

1. **Symmetric layout A (notes 15, 17)**, `LevelUpLayout`: an **inventory column** on the left mirrors the stats column
   on the right: both `StatsW` (410) wide at the same scale `k`, both hanging from `ColumnTop`, at most `StatsH` tall,
   the cards always centred (`ContentX = 0`, `Centred = true`).
   - `margin = (w − row·s)/2 − 2·Edge`; `k = min(1, margin / StatsW)`. Each column sits `Edge` outside the row and at
     least `Edge` inside the screen.
   - `k < MinStats (0.7)`: `k = 0.7` and the row shrinks to fit between the columns:
     `s = min(s, (w − 4·Edge − 2·StatsW·k) / row)`; if that is under `MinCards (0.6)`, `s = 0.6` and the columns shrink
     instead, `k = max(0.5, (w − 4·Edge − row·s) / (2·StatsW))`.
   - Add `InventoryLeft(canvas, fit)` (the column's left edge from the canvas's left) beside `ColumnLeft`. EditMode
     tests: 1920×1080 with 3 and 4 cards, 4:3, 5:4, 21:9 and a short 1280×720: the columns never overlap the row or
     leave the screen, the row is centred, and both columns are mirror images.
   - The pause uses the same fit as a 3-card level-up, so the two screens line up.
2. **`InventoryPanel`** (note 15): a bronze column (`FrameKind.Bronze`, like the stats column) titled in the column's
