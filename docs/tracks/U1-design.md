## 0. U1 in one paragraph
Every screen keeps its layout logic, texts, inputs and test hooks, and changes its **skin**: four fonts in fixed roles,
a small set of colour tokens, and frames drawn as point-filtered pixel art from one atlas: tabula ansata buttons,
carved marble pages, riveted bronze plates, meander bands, mosaic borders, laurel, wax tablets, scutum tiles and the
vexillum banner. Two screens also change **layout**: the level-up (layout A: 300×520 cards centred with the title and
buttons, the stats column in the right margin) and the announcer (a cloth banner that drops, sways, holds and rolls up).
One `UiTheme` asset holds fonts, colours and the frame atlas, so retuning it restyles everything without touching a
screen or rebuilding a scene.

## 1. Rules
1. **Readability first.** Over a fight with 300 enemies, every number and line must read at 720p. Frames sit around
   content, never behind words; nothing decorative moves while the player must read it (the banner moves only while
   dropping, swaying once and rolling up).
2. **Two reds.** Orange-red (`danger`, #FF5433 family) stays reserved for what hurts you: hazards, enemy projectiles,
   telegraphs, damage taken, edicts switched on (R7 decision 11). **Roman red is oxblood crimson** (#7A1F25 / #9B2E33):
   banners, rules under titles, the death screen, banish. Crimson is never used for a thing to dodge; orange-red is
   never decoration. U1 removes the orange-red that isn't danger: the intro's FIGHT!, the boss timer's BOSS, banish
   mode, "YOU DIED".
3. **Laurel means victory.** Beside a word or behind an icon, never behind text, and only on: the victory screen, an
   unlock earned, Legendary cards, a won daily, a beaten arena, the picked gladiator, the Favor icon.
4. **One role, one font.** No faux bold or italic (none of the four fonts has those cuts): emphasis is size, colour or
   the role. `UiFactory.Text`'s default Bold is not used by themed text.
5. **Pixel scale.** Frames are drawn at **3 canvas units per art pixel**: whole screen pixels at 1080p (3) and 720p
   (2). Icons stay in 20-unit steps (today's rule).
6. **No third-party art.** Frames are generated by a script (`PixelSrc/ui_frames.py`, like `upgrade_icons.py`); fonts
   are the producer's four free fonts with their licences shipped.
7. **Same words, same inputs.** No text, key or button changes except those listed here (§5, §6); every test hook
   (`CardTitle`, `CardRect`, `Content`, `EdictsButtonText`, …) keeps its meaning.

### 2.3 Font assets
- **Static SDF atlases** (1024², SDFAA, sampling 72 pt, padding 9) with each role's glyph set baked in, so no glyph is
  ever added mid-run (zero GC; today's dynamic atlas relies on each panel's `Presize`).
- **Glyph sets:** Number = digits, A–Z, a–z, space and `+-%:/.,!?[]()'`; Shout = A–Z, a–z, digits, space and
  `!?.,'-:`; Title and Body = printable ASCII. The typographic symbols the game writes (`×·—–…→←↑↓’“”•−°²÷±≈√Σ∓◀▶■`:
  "→" in every stat preview, "×" in every multiplier) are baked into each font that has them and into a small static
  **`FN_Symbols`** atlas made from LiberationSans; every theme font falls back to `FN_Symbols`, then to LiberationSans
  SDF (dynamic, last resort). A test lists any required glyph that none of them has.
- **Looks** (TMP material presets per font): **Flat**; **Outline** (dark ink #1A0F08 rim + drop shadow underlay: text
  over the 3D scene); **Engraved** (dark ink with a light 1-unit underlay below: text cut into marble or a bronze
  button face).
- **Licences:** Apache 2.0 (Luckiest Guy) and OFL 1.1 (the other three) allow embedding in a paid game. Each font's
  licence file sits beside it in `Assets/Art/Fonts/<Family>/` and is copied into the build
  (`StreamingAssets/Licenses/Fonts/`); `docs/CREDITS.md` names the fonts, authors and licences.

## 7. The announcer
### 7.1 The banner (`VexillumBanner`)
A bronze crossbar (≈ 820×24, gold finials) hangs under the boss bar (its top at y 204 from the screen's top); the
cloth (760 wide, ≈ 120 tall with the swallowtail hem) hangs from it inside a mask, so it appears from behind the bar.

| Phase | Time (UiTheme) | Motion |
|---|---|---|
| Drop | 0.32 s | from fully behind the bar (−104 % of its height) down to +5 %, ease-out |
| Settle | 0.12 s | +5 % → −2 % → 0 |
| Sway | 0.70 s (inside the hold) | rotation about the bar's centre: +1.4°, −0.9°, +0.4°, 0 (a damped swing) |
| Hold | 2.0 s, or the event's `Seconds` if longer (boss lines 3 s) | still |
| Roll up | 0.28 s, ease-in | back behind the bar |

### 7.2 What goes on the banner

| Event | Cloth |
|---|---|
| `DirectorEvent` Swarm / Elite; `AnnounceKind.Whim` | **crimson** |

## 8. Fit rules
### 8.1 The canvas
`UiFactory.Canvas` stays: reference 1920×1080, `ScaleWithScreenSize`, match 0.5. Canvas sizes that result:

| Screen | Examples | Canvas (units) |
|---|---|---|
| 16:9 | 1920×1080, **1280×720**, 2560×1440 | 1920×1080 (720p = the same picture at ⅔) |
| **16:10** | 1920×1200, Steam Deck 1280×800 | ≈ 1822×1138 |
| **4:3** | 1600×1200, 1024×768 | ≈ 1663×1247 |
| 21:9 (not a target; must not break) | 2560×1080 | ≈ 2217×935 |

So: **widths down to 1663, heights down to 935.** Everything is anchored to an edge or the centre; blocks that don't
fit scale down, never up (as `MenuLayout` already does).

### 8.2 General
- No text under 18 units (12 px at 720p); body text ≥ 20; HUD numbers ≥ 20.
- Marcellus is wider than LiberationSans: every fixed label box is checked at 1920×1080; labels that can grow get
  autosize (floor 18) and `Truncate`, **never Ellipsis**.
- Frames at 3 units per art pixel; frame sizes in multiples of 3 where it's free.
- Modals (Edicts 1640×980, Daily 1760×790, Codex, options) scale by `min(1, (W − 2m) / w, (H − 2m) / h)`, m = 24.

### 8.3 The level-up
1. Card row width `R = n × 300 + (n − 1) × 28` (956 for three, 1284 for four); content height ≈ 900 (title top to
   buttons' bottom).
2. Content scale `s = min(1, (H − 40) / 900)` (21:9: 0.994).
3. Right margin `M = (W − R·s) / 2 − 2 × 24` (the screen edge and the gap to the cards). The stats column (410)
   scales to `k = min(1, M / 410)` while `k ≥ 0.7`: the cards stay **centred**.
4. If `k` would fall under 0.7: `k = 0.7`, and the cards shift left until they clear the column by 24, then shrink
   (floor 0.6) if they still don't fit (today's behaviour).

| Case | Result |
|---|---|
| 16:9, 3 cards | centred, column 410 (k = 1) |
| 16:10, 3 cards | centred, k ≈ 0.94 |
| 4:3, 3 cards | centred, k ≈ 0.75 |
| 16:9, 4 cards | k = 0.7, cards shifted left ≈ 17, unscaled |
| 4:3, 4 cards | k = 0.7, cards shifted left ≈ 146, unscaled |

### 8.4 HUD
Corners are anchored; the top-left plate, the centre timer and the top-right cluster need ≈ 1,050 units: fine at
4:3. The banner (≤ 820 wide) and callouts (box 1,100, autosize) fit 1663.

## 9. Performance and GC
- **Zero allocation per frame in a run** (CLAUDE.md §4), unchanged. Static font atlases (no glyph adds), every in-run
  label still pre-sized, constant strings for the banner, colour/alpha/position changes only.
- **The banner and the card icons move every frame**: the banner lives on its own nested `Canvas` so its motion
  re-batches only itself (the damage numbers already have one).
- **One frame atlas** (one texture, point-filtered) for every frame: the HUD's frames batch together; today each
  `PixelIcons` sprite is its own texture.
- Materials: four fonts × three looks = at most 12 TMP materials, UI only; no scene material is added.
- Shown / hidden with `CanvasGroup` as today (never `SetActive`, never `Canvas.enabled`).

## 10. Decisions (one-line reasons)
1. **`UiTheme` lives in `Assets/Resources/` and is read at runtime (`UiTheme.Current`).** No scene rebuild: six arenas,
   the menu and the dev scenes keep their old `font` fields (now unused), and nothing needs re-linking.
2. **Frames come from a Python pixel generator into one atlas + index** (like `upgrade_icons.py`). The art worker can
   render and look at them without Unity, they're diffable and regenerable, and one texture keeps the UI batching.
3. **Sprites are cut from the atlas at runtime** (`Sprite.Create` with borders from the index). No sprite-sheet import
   setup or 2D Sprite package; the importer only sets point filter / no mips / no compression.
4. **3 canvas units per art pixel.** Whole pixels at both 1080p and 720p.
5. **Static TMP atlases + a static `FN_Symbols` fallback.** Marcellus and Luckiest Guy may lack "→" or "×"; the
   symbols atlas draws them with no glyph added mid-run (zero GC), and a test lists anything still missing.
6. **Themed builders are new (`UiKit`); `UiFactory` is untouched.** The admin panel and anything not restyled keep
   today's look and compile unchanged; each screen moves to `UiKit` on its own.
7. **No faux bold / italic.** The four fonts have no such cuts; TMP's fake styles smear them.
8. **The banner is a plain ticked class (like `UpgradeIconView`) built by A, routed by B.** A can finish and test it in
   the gallery alone; B owns the HUD's rules (priority, callouts).
9. **Banner routing by `AnnounceKind`, not by the sender's colour.** Senders don't change; a whim's danger colour or a
   boss's reveal colour can't send a line to the wrong cloth.
10. **Streaks, roars, close calls, gifts and lines stay callouts.** They come every few seconds in a big fight; a
    curtain each time would bury the swarm, the elite and the boss.
11. **All four card frames share one inset with a 24-unit foot.** One anatomy for every rarity; the Rare's meander
    and the materials live in the foot and the rim, never over text.
12. **Card icon stays 100 (5 units per art pixel).** It's the producer's centrepiece; layout A's 88 isn't a 20-step,
    and the height budget (§6.2) still leaves 15 units.
13. **The stats column keeps layout A's Body font for values;** Luckiest Guy is for numbers that are the element.
14. **Roster tiles become scutum shields carrying the portrait in a clipeus.** The producer picked the scutum for
    gladiator tiles; the clipeus (the Roman portrait shield) keeps the face the centre of the tile, as R9 wanted.
15. **Bronze = the screen's main action, marble = the rest.** The mockup's FIGHT! / CODEX pair; the eye finds the one
    forward button.
16. **Pause page on marble, pick column on bronze.** The mockup's "pause page" and layout A; reading quietly vs
    choosing.
17. **YOU DIED on a crimson banner; FIGHT!, BOSS and BANISH leave orange-red.** None of them hurts you.
18. **The boss reward reads "SPOILS OF VICTORY".** Today every boss's reward says "THE PIT MASTER'S HOARD" (Gorm's).
19. **Per-arena UI palettes wait** ("later" on the mockup page): one theme first.
